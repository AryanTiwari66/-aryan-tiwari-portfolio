Newsletter Performance Report
September 2026 campaign vs. prior sends
Prepared 15 September 2026 · Source: Mailchimp

The September send reached ~9× more people than any newsletter before it — and still posted our best open rate.

The main September 2026 newsletter went to 10,575 recipients at a 36.3% open rate, versus roughly 860–1,900 recipients on every earlier send. A follow-up resend to non-openers then nearly doubled total link clicks. Here is how this campaign compares with the newsletters that came before it.

What to take from this

Scaling the list did not cost engagement

We sent to ~9× more people yet the open rate rose to 36.3% — the highest of any AdLift newsletter and above the 23.1% benchmark. Bigger reach and better engagement moved together.

The resend is worth keeping

A single follow-up to non-openers added 160 clicks — as many as the main send — for only 7 unsubscribes. Low effort, meaningful incremental engagement.

Absolute clicks are up, click rate is diluted

Combined clicks (313) far exceed earlier sends (avg 64), but click rate per recipient is lower on a much larger, colder list. A stronger single primary CTA could lift it.

Watch list hygiene

Bounce hit 12.2% on the main send (vs 6.9% in early 2025). Cleaning inactive and hard-bouncing addresses will protect deliverability as volume grows.
